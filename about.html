The Story Behind the Strategy

Jeff Igoe didn't become a healthcare marketing expert by accident. He started where every clinic owner starts: on the inside. He founded and operated 18 functional medicine clinics, learning firsthand what works, what doesn't, what vendors can be trusted, and which marketing tactics blow up in court. That experience became the bedrock of everything he builds for clients today.

In 2014, he founded Conscious Health Connections — a concierge digital marketing agency built exclusively for functional medicine and integrative health practices. Unlike generalist agencies that add healthcare to their client roster, CHC was designed from day one around the unique regulatory environment of medical marketing: Anti-Kickback Statute compliance, HIPAA, Google's healthcare advertising policies, and Meta's special ad category restrictions.

Over the years, Jeff has helped 300+ clinic owners across all 50 states build predictable new patient pipelines, diversify their revenue, and scale confidently — generating $275M+ in documented revenue in the process. He has personally managed over $150M in advertising spend across Google Ads, Meta, and Broadcast TV without a single compliance violation.

Recognizing that clinic owners were drowning in bad vendor advice, Jeff created The Igoe Score — a proprietary 0–10 rating system that independently scores functional medicine products and vendors across 6 weighted categories. Built from hard-earned experience and backed by a 300+ doctor network, the Igoe Score has become the industry's trusted reference for purchasing decisions.

To address the single biggest vulnerability in most medical practices — dependence on a single revenue stream — Jeff developed The Igoe Stack: a multi-revenue diversification framework refined across 300+ practices and 18 personally operated clinics. Practitioners who implement the Igoe Stack typically add 2–4 new revenue streams within 90–120 days.

Most recently, Jeff co-founded Saara AI, an AI operating system purpose-built for modern healthcare clinics. Saara OS provides closed-loop patient management, AI-driven marketing automation, compliance-first architecture (AKS, HIPAA, Google, Meta), and real-time revenue cycle intelligence — bringing enterprise-level efficiency to independent practices.

Jeff's philosophy is simple: healthcare marketing must be compliance-first, results-second — and you never have to choose between the two. His track record proves it.
