Official Bio for Media Use

Jeff Igoe is a strategic marketing operations leader and Fractional CMO with 21+ years of experience in functional medicine and integrative health marketing. He has generated over $275 million in revenue for 300+ clinic owners across all 50 states, personally managed $150M+ in advertising spend, and launched or operated 18 functional medicine clinics himself.

Jeff is the Founder of Conscious Health Connections (2014), the Creator of the Igoe Score — the only independently scored vendor rating system for functional medicine — the developer of the Igoe Stack revenue diversification framework, and Co-Founder of Saara AI, a medically-focused artificial intelligence company.

His expertise spans compliant Google Ads and Meta advertising, Broadcast TV for healthcare, GoHighLevel automation, Anti-Kickback Statute compliance, HIPAA-compliant marketing systems, and AI-powered clinic operations.
